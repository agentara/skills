# Brand, Product, and Technology Styles

These styles support commercial, product, and technology posters with readable copy and strong visual hierarchy.

## creator-economy-brand

- Core keywords: expressive avatar shapes, modular social cards, punchy colors, sticker interface elements, camera cues, microphone cues, energetic digital business mood
- Prompt add-on: Create a creator economy brand poster with expressive avatar shapes, modular social cards, punchy colors, sticker-like interface elements, camera or microphone cues, and energetic digital business mood.
- Best for: creator tools, personal brands, AI creators, newsletters, launch visuals.
- Avoid: bank-like corporate stiffness, antique print, fake app UI details.

## tech-launch-interface

- Core keywords: product launch, interface fragments, luminous grid, precise typography, data aura, device glow, clean future
- Prompt add-on: Build a future-facing tech launch poster with a hero model/product name, luminous grid, interface fragments as abstract texture, precise sans or mono typography, and controlled glow.
- Best for: AI models, developer tools, software releases, technical milestones.
- Avoid: dense dashboards, random code, invented UI facts.

## product-launch-hero

- Core keywords: hero product, macro detail, commercial gloss, clean shadow, launch badge, brand color fields
- Prompt add-on: Create a product launch hero poster with a dominant macro product/object view, polished lighting, clean shadow, launch badge, brand-color fields, and concise readable feature labels only when supplied.
- Best for: devices, objects, packages, vehicles, collectibles.
- Avoid: fake certification badges, unprovided specs, bland catalog layout.
