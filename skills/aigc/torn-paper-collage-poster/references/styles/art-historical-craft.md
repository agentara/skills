# Art, Historical, Regional, and Craft Styles

These styles translate established visual traditions into poster-ready language without copying specific protected works.

## museum-exhibition-editorial

- Core keywords: museum exhibition, art-book layout, archival object, refined grid, curator label, quiet prestige
- Prompt add-on: Create a museum exhibition poster with refined art-book grid, one object or artwork-like image, curator label typography, quiet prestige, and readable date/venue fields only if supplied.
- Best for: galleries, exhibitions, lectures, cultural institutions.
- Avoid: fake museum logos, invented provenance, loud marketing badges.

## art-nouveau-botanical

- Core keywords: art nouveau, botanical frame, flowing linework, ornate title, organic ornament
- Prompt add-on: Use art nouveau-inspired poster language with flowing botanical linework, ornamental frame, elegant organic title, soft decorative color, and a clear central subject.
- Best for: beauty, music, gardens, cultural events, graceful brands.
- Avoid: copying specific historic posters, over-ornament that hides text.

## art-deco-luxe

- Core keywords: art deco, sunburst geometry, metallic accent, symmetrical luxury, elegant headline
- Prompt add-on: Build an art deco luxe poster with symmetrical geometry, sunburst forms, metallic accents, tall elegant display type, and theatrical premium contrast.
- Best for: luxury, nightlife, architecture, fashion, premium events.
- Avoid: fake gold excess, clutter, illegible thin type.

## ukiyoe-modern

- Core keywords: ukiyo-e, flat waves, woodblock texture, modern subject, Japanese print rhythm
- Prompt add-on: Create a modern ukiyo-e-inspired poster with flat wave or cloud rhythm, woodblock texture, strong contour shapes, limited color, and modern subject integration.
- Best for: travel, culture, products, music, nature themes.
- Avoid: copying known prints, random kanji-like text, cultural pastiche without purpose.

## chinese-ink-poster

- Core keywords: Chinese ink, brush texture, white space, seal accent, contemporary oriental poster
- Prompt add-on: Use contemporary Chinese ink poster language with expressive brush texture, generous white space, seal-like accent, restrained color, and elegant readable title placement.
- Best for: cultural stories, rural scenes, food, craft, historical topics.
- Avoid: fake calligraphy text, decorative dragons by default, overloaded red/gold.

## soviet-constructivist

- Core keywords: Soviet constructivist, red black cream, heroic angle, diagonal blocks, propaganda poster
- Prompt add-on: Build a constructivist poster with heroic angle, diagonal red/black/cream blocks, bold sans or slab typography, strong directional arrows, and movement-like urgency.
- Best for: movements, technology, sports, historical or ironic campaigns.
- Avoid: real political insignia, extremist symbols, copied propaganda layouts.

## midcentury-modern

## psychedelic-60s
